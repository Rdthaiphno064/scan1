Peer pressure has both good and bad effects on us.
First, it can help us build good habits.
When our friends study hard, we want to study hard too.
When they exercise every day, we join them and become healthier.
This helps us get better marks at school.
Second, peer pressure helps us feel part of a group.
We make more friends and feel happy around them.
We also learn how to talk and work with other people.
It motivates us to set goals and work hard to reach them.
However, peer pressure also has bad sides.
Some people start to smoke or drink because their friends do it.
This is very bad for their health.
Also, we may feel very stressed and worried about what others think of us.
We try hard to fit in and forget who we really are.
In the end, we stop making our own choices and just follow others.
