There are many ways we can protect nature around us.
First, we must make strong rules to stop people from hunting and selling wild animals.
When we do this, many animals can live safely in nature.
Second, we should plant more native trees and flowers in our gardens, parks, and schools.
Native plants give animals food and a safe place to live.
They also make our neighborhoods look more beautiful and green.
Third, we need to use fewer chemicals on our farms and in our gardens.
Too many chemicals can harm the soil and water.
When the soil and water are clean, plants and animals can grow well.
Finally, we should teach more people about nature.
Schools and local groups can run programs and campaigns to help people learn.
When people know more, they will care more and do more to protect the plants and animals around them.
Every small action helps.
